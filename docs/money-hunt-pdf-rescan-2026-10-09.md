# The Money Hunt — native-resolution rescan of the book PDF (2026-10-09)

**Source:** `money-hunt-book.pdf` (EPSON Scan, PDF 1.4, 32 pages). Every page carries a
**native 2492×3289 JPEG — a true 300 dpi scan** of the 598×789 pt page (≈8.3″×11″). That is
**twice the linear resolution** of the ~1246 px renders behind the 2026-10-05 state analysis,
and it is uniform: the earlier renders were 150 dpi for pages 1–12 and 300 dpi only for the
clue pages 13–28, while the PDF gives full 300 dpi for all 32 pages. The native JPEGs were
extracted without re-rendering and re-read by vision at native resolution, with 3–6×
LANCZOS zooms on micro-targets. (The extracted page images live outside this repository, in
the private `karen_puzzles/money hunt puzzle/pdf_extract_300dpi/` folder, with working tiles
and zoom crops in its `crops/` folder — they are Karen's scans, not ours to republish.)

**Verdict on "is the PDF worth scanning":** yes — it is the best available source and it
settled several standing unknowns below. But it is 300 dpi, not 600: hand-drawn micro-text
(necklace beads, the p16 calculator display) still hits the resolution floor. The remaining
open micro-targets are listed at the end; only those still justify a 600-dpi pass, and only
a re-scan of the physical book can provide it.

## Findings

### 1. Page 18 — the Braille does NOT exist on this page
All four quadrants were searched at native resolution, plus 3× zooms on the only two
candidate dot-rows (both are decorative stippling / nail heads along the floor planks, with
no 2×3 cell structure). **No Braille cells anywhere on page 18.** The "braille on a
baseboard = staff s 5 f" attribution in Karen's video walkthrough repeats the same
mis-filing as the withdrawn page note — it cannot be this page. The STAFF S5_F string
remains un-homed; pages checked negative for Braille so far: 10, 13, 16, 18, 27, 28.
Bonus confirmations on p18: jars CANDY / COOKIES / JELLY BEANS, bottle HOUSE WINE, milk
cartons 7 OZ / 8 OZ / 16 OZ, vertical "Wea…" at top right, explosion letters W S N O I R
plus a small B — consistent with the BROWNS anagram reading.

### 2. Page 5 — missing 4 confirmed on BOTH dials; 7:28 plausible but unpinned
The pocket watch and the square wristwatch both carry Roman-numeral dials, **neither dial
shows any form of 4 (no IV, no IIII)**, and the numerals are additionally scrambled (e.g.
II printed near the 10 o'clock position on the wristwatch). The square wristwatch's hands
sit low-left — hour hand at VII/VIII, minute hand around V/VI — i.e. **roughly 7:25–7:35,
consistent with Karen's 7:28 reading but not pinned** (the hands are sketchy drawings; at
4× zoom no exact minute is defensible). The pocket watch reads closer to X–XI hands, so the
old "10:09" note stays unverifiable. Necklace best read at double the prior resolution:
**H O O Y I P ? S ? I H P** — consistent with HOOYIPSHIP, with two beads still
unresolvable at 300 dpi.
*Implication:* the deliberate absence of 4 on both dials is now confirmed, which keeps the
7:28 ↔ 28 July 1945 crash-date hypothesis alive as a page-5 confirmation device.

### 3. Page 13 — the telescope is HERE (not p27); aimed at the inverted building
A brass **tripod spyglass/telescope stands in the upper-left sky area (~0.13 W, 0.09 H of
the page)**, angled upward toward the **inverted building/castle** in the sky. No airplane
in or on the telescope, and no aircraft anywhere on pages 10, 13 or 27. Karen's remembered
"telescope looking at a head-on airplane" is therefore a **composite memory**: the
telescope and inverted sky-building (both p13) plus the airplane motif (front cover; also
the toy-plane motif family). The p13 cluster — an observer's telescope aimed at an inverted
building in the sky, above a painter at work — is now the book's clearest "look at the
building" image and fits the Empire State Building reading without requiring an airplane.
Also confirmed on p13: **FIRST CLASS 2 / FIRST CLASS 7 / FIRST CLASS (digit hidden)**
luggage tags, license plate "01", arched stone bridge, waterfall.

### 4. Page 16 — second calculator CONFIRMED; "MADE IN CAN" truncation is deliberate
- A **calculator lies at bottom-right (~0.80 W, 0.90 H) among winter boots/shoes** — the
  video detail is verified. Its display is too occluded to read even at 5× zoom (up to six
  digit positions glimpsed; no reliable reading). This is the book's second calculator
  after page 5's 1,000,000,000.
- The shaft label reads **MADE IN CAN** — at 6× zoom there is no continuation; the label
  simply ends (individual tubes above a bag are lettered, e.g. W / A / S). The truncation
  is deliberate, not a scan crop. The Canada signal stands.
- Also seen: Fe26-Worker-style trophy plaque, locker "7°/8°", a "000000"-style device, a
  "SEASON …" box, ski/skate/curling gear. The day strip (W T F S S M T W T F S S M T W T
  over 1×6 then 2×10) is unchanged.

### 5. Page 27 — no telescope, no airplane
The music-room inventory stands (JAZZ letters, MUSIC ROOM banner, STYNBANG piano, VOLUME/
TON dials, candlestick phone, monitor). The transcript's attribution of the telescope to
p27 was a second-hand summary error.

### 6. Page 10 — no airplane; suitcase glyphs fixed at a 2×4 grid; clue 17 completed
- No telescope or aircraft anywhere on the resort-pool page; the withdrawn note's "model
  airplane diving at a building" is definitively not this page. The closest real object in
  the book is p13's telescope + inverted building.
- The suitcase glyph grid is a **2-row × 4-column grid** (8 glyphs), alternating "3 +
  triangle" forms and zigzag / M-Σ-like glyphs — still undecoded, but the grid shape is
  now fixed (it is not a longer string).
- The DOWN clue list (1–37) is confirmed again, and **clue 17 now reads in full:
  "Ethelbert ———, American composer 1862-1901, wrote Mighty Lak' a Rose"** → the answer is
  NEVIN. (Clue 8's "1927 song hit from Rodgers and Hart's *A Connecticut Yankee*" → THOU
  SWELL, another now-solvable entry.)

### 7. Page 28 — spines and volume row re-confirmed
No telescope. Native-resolution spine reads broadly confirm the errata list (FLAMINGCOES
TODAY, CALL OF THE MILD, HOW TO PAINT NAILS, 50 WAYS TO LEAVE YOUR MOTHER, CRASH DIETING —
The Stock Market, MEMOIRS OF A MONKEY, plus a CELEBRATED SIN… garble whose middle remains
ambiguous — SINSORS/SINUSES family). The volume row **23, 19, 21, 20, 22, 18…9,
DICTIONARY N-Z, 7…2, DICTIONARY A-M** is re-confirmed exactly, as is the G. BAGE desk box
with **VOL 149145**, the eagle, and the framed prices ($5537 / $16M / $334 / $618 region).
