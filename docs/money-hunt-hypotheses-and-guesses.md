# The Money Hunt ($1,000,000, 1986) — Macro/Micro Synthesis, Dead Ends & Candidate Guesses

This document consolidates everything learned across the clue-book scans, artifact
photographs, court filings, 1986 newspaper coverage, Karen Puzzles' video & solving
notes, and all ~1,238 YouTube comments and replies into one structured reference for
future human solvers and future AI models.

> **Why higher-resolution scans are NOT needed:** `money-hunt-book.pdf` already provides
> native **300-dpi (`2492×3289`)** renders of all 32 pages. Artist **K. S. Perry's**
> illustrations were hand-drawn in ink and assembled by physical cut-and-paste /
> photostat reduction. Ambiguous micro-marks (such as two tiny strokes under `p18`'s
> `HEAD` strip or plain dial circles on `p9`) sit at the **precision ceiling of the
> original drawing**, not of the scanner. Progress requires **zooming out** (page
> sequence, production history, structural rules) and **zooming in** (cross-page object
> logic and unsolved codes on existing renders)—not higher DPI.

### 1.3 Five Macro Tensions Every Theory Must Account For

1. **The Nov 1985 "Mr. S" Split & "Last-Minute Solution Swap" Possibility:**
   Court filings show the puzzle constructor ("Mr. S", hired via a Mensa ad) worked ~2
   years on the 24 pages, then in **November 1985** demanded $10,000 + royalties and
   **refused to hand over the solution**. Creator **John Poser** / Trivia Games Inc.
   nonetheless delivered a sealed answer to **Touche Ross & Co.** by **February 26, 1986**
   and published in **May 1986**—just 3 to 6 months after losing the designer's key!
   * *Consequence:* Either (a) Poser/Perry already knew the intended destination (which
     Poser's son recalled involved the **July 28, 1945 B-25 crash into the Empire State
     Building**) and bolted on the jigsaw poem + poster trail to make sure players could
     reach it, or (b) some Mr. S page-level sub-puzzles (`p7` knitting, `p18` under-specified
     crossword, `p24` wheel) are orphaned relics of Mr. S's unrecovered master chain.
2. **Evidence of Two Box Designs & Cut Prototype Clues:**
   * A commenter (`@5kmrunder15`) reported an earliest box design **without** a jigsaw
     puzzle; on Karen's retail box back, `*Also included is a 150 piece... jigsaw puzzle`
     carries an **unmatched leading asterisk**—classic paste-up evidence of an added line.
   * The artist who painted the **prototype box cover** wrote in the YouTube comments that
     he believed **serial numbers on the prototype cover's dollar bills were a clue**,
     which vanished when the retail cover was simplified to solid green.
3. **Entry-Page Contradiction (Page 11 vs. Page 15):**
   * The **jigsaw border** reads: *"To start the game go to page eleven and find the queen
     of spades, and look for other clues…"*, and the jigsaw poem ends *"To start the game,
     find the place one did play."*
   * Yet the **flip-side poster** places the **START flag on Page 15** (a nursery/playroom—
     literally *"the place one did play"*!) and puts **Page 11 last** at the arrow into
     the money pile.
4. **"24 Clues" (Lawsuit) vs. "More Than One Clue Per Page" (Rulebook) vs. Page 31 Log:**
   * The court filing refers to *"24 clues"* (one per folio `5–28`), whereas page 2 warns
     there is *"more than one clue per page"* to block lucky guesses, and **Page 31
     (Player's Log)** gives **only 3 short lines per page**—matching a compact per-page
     output (such as `State` / `Key Word` / `Letter or Number`), not ten separate answers.
5. **The Kit Williams *Masquerade* Precedent:**
   * Poser modeled the contest on *Masquerade* (1979), which was famous for packing pages
     with solvable-looking red-herring ciphers while hiding the true answer in a simple
     cross-page geometric/index rule (lines drawn from eyes through fingers to border
     letters).

## 2. Zooming In — Hard-Verified Micro-Clues & Numbers

* **Empire State Building Crash Cluster (July 28, 1945, 9:40–9:49 AM, 14 killed, 78th–80th floors):**
  * **Jigsaw border poem:** *"Climb way up high, not quite to the sky, a sad story was
    told, of one not very old, with lives made to pay, it happened one day, the mist was
    around, no way to be found…"* (27-year-old pilot Lt. Col. William F. Smith Jr. flew a
    B-25 Mitchell in heavy fog into the 78th–80th floors—high up, *"not quite to the sky"*—
    killing **14** people).
  * **Page 28 desk box:** `Life & Times of G. Bage / Vol 149145` → splits cleanly into
    **`14`** (lives lost), **`9[:45]`** (crash time ~9:40–9:45 AM), **`1945`** (year)—or
    `14-9-14-5` (`N-I-N-E`).
  * **Page 5 watches:** Both dials omit any form of **`4`** (echoed on `p23` by additive
    stanchion `IIII`); wristwatch hands sit around **`7:25–7:35`** (`7:28` ↔ **July 28**).
  * **Page 13 tripod spyglass:** Aimed upward at an **inverted building in the sky**
    (strongest pictorial echo of the skyscraper/sky disaster after `p1`'s head-on plane).
  * **Page 30 registration address:** `P.O. Box 803878` in Chicago (contains both **`80`**
    and **`78`**, the crash floors—possibly coincidence, possibly chosen vanity box).
* **Proven Multi-Step Page Templates:**
  * **Page 12 (`WASHINGTON`):** Rotate landscape art 90° CW → count birds of each of 10
    species → take the *N*th letter of that species' name → top-to-bottom spells
    `WASHINGTON`.
  * **Page 13 ↔ Page 20 (`LINE` / `LINES`):** Identical parking `P` sign is plain on `p13`
    and crossed by a diagonal slash on `p20`; `p11` DOWN 46 *"Queues"* = `LINES`.
  * **Page 28 Shelf Instructions:** Volumes `19` and `22` are swapped (`23, 19, 21, 20,
    22, 18`), `DICTIONARY N–Z` sits in slot `8` while `DICTIONARY A–M` sits in slot `1`,
    and spine `VA DERS` drops `IN` from `INVADERS`.
* **Confirmed Deliberate Misspellings (Scan-Verified Set):**
  * `p6`: `AWITHIAKNANNAI` (`I`←`L`), `Solitoier` (`oi`←`ai`), `PARCHESI` (missing `E`)
  * `p17`: `Home Sveet Home` (`V`←`W`)
  * `p23`: `Sylvestor` (`o`←`e`, clue 142)
  * `p27`: `STYNBANG` (`YNBANG`←`EINWAY`)
  * `p28`: `Cinderellow` (`ow`←`a`), `Konnan The Librarian` (`Konn`←`Con` + gag word),
    `HUCKLEBERRY GRINS` (`GRINS`←`FINN`), `50 WAYS TO LEAVE YOUR MOTHER` (`MOTHER`←`LOVER`),
    `CALL OF THE MILD` (`M`←`W`), `Cnocking with Winners` (`C`←`K`).

## 4. Possible Solution Mechanisms (Open High-Level Frameworks & Human Leads)

### Mechanism M1 — Two-Layer Extraction: Per-Page State Elimination + Crash-Site Specifics
* **Idea:** All 24 clue pages (or 23 pages + 1 key page) encode a **US state or Canadian
  province** (like `p12 = WASHINGTON`, `p14 = FLORIDA`, `p24 = ONTARIO`, `p27 = LOUISIANA`),
  either eliminating 24 wrong jurisdictions or feeding a check table on Page 31's 3-line
  log, while a second layer (the jigsaw poem + cross-page meta-objects + `p5`/`p13`/`p28`
  crash numbers) pins down `New York` and the exact site inside the Empire State Building.
* **What's needed to advance it:** Cracking how non-pictorial code pages (`p15` math/music,
  `p18` mini-grid + 25 letters, `p24` wheel, `p10` `2×4` glyphs) output a state name.

### Mechanism M2 — The "Last-Minute T.G.I. Override" (Jigsaw + Poster Trail)
* **Idea:** After Mr. S withheld his intricate 24-page master chain in November 1985,
  Poser/Perry added the **150-piece jigsaw** (note the `*` on the box back) and the
  **flip-side trail poster** between Nov 1985 and Feb 1986 so the game remained solvable
  toward Poser's chosen destination without Mr. S's private notebook.
* **Testable prediction:** Under M2, the **jigsaw poem**, **Page 11 (`Q♠`)**, **Page 15
  (`START` flag = "place one did play")**, and the **most transparent visual clues** (`p5`
  `7:28`, `p28` `Vol 149145`, `p13` spyglass at sky-building) carry the *entire* intended
  post-split solve, while dense orphan grids like `p7`'s knitting pattern or `p18`'s
  under-specified crossword are leftover Mr. S art!

### Mechanism M3 — Cross-Page Cut-and-Paste Object Assembly ("The Assembled Bell & Props")
* **Idea:** Multiple pages share physically cut-up or photocopied pieces of one object:
  * **The Bell:** Body + urn on `p26`, clapper/ribbon on `p9`, ribbon + `O '3`/`g' 3` on
    `p18`, bell lantern on `p19`.
  * **Paired Props:** `p13/p20` `P`-signs (`LINE`), `p15/p19` clown (`7` shade vs cap),
    `p11/p26` 3-card hand (`Q♠, 3♣, J♥`), `p17/p27` telephone + cord, `p7/p11` `FLAMER
    TAMER` book, `p5/p16` calculators.
* **Testable prediction:** Overlaying or ordering ONLY the subset of pages carrying pieces
  of the **Bell** (or pairing the 6–8 true twin-prop pages using `p28`'s `19↔22` swap
  rule!) yields a short phrase or location (`LIBERTY BELL`, `HERALD SQUARE`—where the
  famous **James Gordon Bennett Monument / Minerva & Bell-Ringers clock** stands one block
  from the Empire State Building at 34th & Broadway!—WAIT: note that **Herald Square** at
  34th St. & Broadway, literally at the foot of the Empire State Building, is famous for
  its giant bronze **Bell** and clock-ringers "Stuff and Guff"!).

### Mechanism M4 — *Masquerade*-Style Geometric / Sight-Line Construction
* **Idea:** Following Kit Williams's *Masquerade*, lines drawn from pointing/gazing figures
  (`p13` spyglass → inverted building, `p21` 15 saluting hands, `p14` pointing hurricane/peninsula,
  `p11` poolside gaze) or connecting page thumbnails on the **flip-side poster** trace
  letters, compass bearings, or a landmark silhouette.
