## SUMMARY OF THE MONEY HUNT — OFFICIAL RULES AND CONDITIONS

1. Each game purchase allows you **three (3) answers**; three official numbered answer forms per game; one answer per form. No copies accepted.
2. Answer forms to: **THE MONEY HUNT contest headquarters, P.O. Box 34973, Seattle WA, 98124-1973**, no later than **March 31, 1988**.
3. No responsibility for delayed/lost/destroyed entries or registration recording errors; entrant must verify receipt and recording.
4. **$1,000,000 (U.S.) to the person(s) who correctly identifies the specific reward location.** Paid **$50,000/year for 20 years**; taxes are the winner's responsibility; multiple correct answers share the reward per the payout schedule.
5. **If no correct answer by March 31, 1988: additional clues will be made available and new deadlines provided "until a winner is declared."** No entrant–company communication before then. **The game company does not have access to the official solution, which is held in trust by Touche Ross & Co.**
6. Reward held in trust; awarded after Touche Ross verifies strict rule compliance.
7. **The reward annuity was purchased by Rush Energy Corp., a public company trading on the Vancouver Stock Exchange.**
8. Annuity placed in trust by Rush Energy Corp. solely to be awarded to winner(s); skill-testing examination may be required by law; publicity consent required.
9. Decisions of Touche Ross & Co. and the panel of judges are final. Touche Ross (international firm of independent public accountants) has custody of the official solution during the contest.
10. Open to all natural persons purchasing the original game, except employees/representatives/**the inventor**/agents of the game company, judging organization, accounting firm, and their immediate families. Void where prohibited.
11. Reward awarded equally to each properly registered owner whose answer **exactly matches the official solution**. One correct answer accepted per registered owner. **Don't forget to mail in your registration form.** Address changes to: MONEY HUNT REWARD: $1,000,000, P.O. Box 803878, Chicago, Illinois 60680-3878.

- "Exactly matches the official solution" — implies a precise canonical answer string (likely "place one did play" — a specific named location).
