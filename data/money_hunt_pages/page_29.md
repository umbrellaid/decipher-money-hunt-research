## 🔑 KEY STRUCTURAL FACT
The required answer format is exactly two fields:
1. **State / Province** (the form literally labels the first blank "State/Province")
2. **Specifically at** — a specific place within it

This **confirms** the community's one-state/province-per-page theory shape: the solution must bottom out in a named US state or Canadian province plus a specific location ("the place one did play" per the jigsaw poem).

## Analysis hooks
- "Specifically at" strongly suggests a named venue/landmark — consistent with the Empire State Building lead (New York + a specific spot, e.g., the 79th floor offices of the War Relief Society).
