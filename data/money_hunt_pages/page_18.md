# Money Hunt Clue Book — Page 18 — full transcription

**Status: fully transcribed 2026-10-09 from the native 300-dpi page (2492×3289, extracted
from the book PDF). Text is separated from scene-artwork sketch lines. Supersedes all
earlier page-18 notes; the old "fish shop / Braille / moose head" note describes a
different page (see the errata).**

Scene: a kitchen-counter / fish-stall illustration. Salmon and fish on a counter, jars
and bottles on shelves, an explosion graphic, five bowling-style lanes with dots at lower
left, a rowboat, and a small crossword grid with its clue list on the left wall.

Notes on the list:

  10× zoom). The earlier "Blackjam = deliberate misspelling" claim is **refuted**; that
  entry leaves the book's misspelling collection.
- The separator in line 12 is a middot: "Nap·12". Either "Nap" is clue 11 with "12" the
  number of a faint, unrecovered next line, or "Nap·12" is one garbled line. No "Nap"
  answer fits any grid entry (see solve state below).
- The book's A/D direction labels do **not** match the drawn grid's geometry (cell 1 is
  down-only in the grid, yet is labeled 1A; the "opposite of live" and "be rid of"
  answers seat one slot off from the printed numbers 2/3). The grid itself is drawn

## The "HEAD" columns (upper-middle-left, previously unresolved)

Three vertical columns of the word **HEAD** tiled in a phase shift:

```
col 1: A D H E A D
col 2: E A D H E A
col 3: E A D H E (+ two tiny marks below)
```

i.e. an endless `…HEADHEADHEAD…` ribbon cut into three vertical strips. Given the five
bowling lanes on the same page, "HEAD" plausibly points at the **head pin** — or at
"taking the head (first letter)" of something. Unresolved; now at least fully read.

## Explosion letters

The starburst by the upper right carries **W R B N S O** — exactly the six letters of
**BROWNS** (the B is printed small, beside the W). There is **no I** among them (an
earlier read listed one). Anagram → **BROWNS** — a Cleveland Browns / Ohio tie-in is a
speculative state candidate, unchanged from the state analysis.

## Solve state (exhaustive check, 2026-10-09)

**Intended fill — left cluster locked by crossings:**
- **1D = SAFAR** (Muhammadan month) — or RAJAB, but RAJAB strands 3A (no J-starting answer)
- **2A = SCRAP** (be rid of)
- **3A = AMORT** (opposite of live — archaic for "lifeless, dead")
- These three interlock: SCRAP's A crosses SAFAR, AMORT's A crosses SAFAR, AMORT's O
  forces 4D to start with O.

**Right chain (crossing-locked once 7D = BASIC):**
- **7D = BASIC** (Blackjack term — basic strategy)
- → **10A = CAR** (yuppie's staple; C forced by BASIC)
- → **11D = RIVERHORSE** (10 letters, R-forced; clued by the **hippopotamus in the
  scene** — Greek *hippos* horse + *potamos* river. The book's "more than one clue per
  page" warning in action: a drawn object is the clue.)
- → **13A = ICING** (I-forced; clued by the **"Candy" jar** in the scene, and/or
  "extra special" = the icing on the cake)

**The rest does not resolve cleanly from the printed clues:**
- 4D is forced to start with **O** — no clue answer fits (O????).
- 6A is forced to **??B??** (BASIC's B) — no clue answer fits.
- 5A must end in **R** — the only fit is **DEAR** ("extra special"), which is plausible
  rather than certain.
- Best fill of the remaining sub-chain {6D, 8A, 9D, 12A} places at most 4 of the 5
  remaining clues; one consistent choice is **8A=NEAT / 9D=EEK / 12A=DOCK / 6D=ABUT**
  (undiluted + gasp + harbor + meet), but 3-clue variants cross just as legally
  (8A = DECK/PORT/COVE/MOOR…), so the middle is **not unique**.
- "prices & wrists" and "Nap" fit **no** entry in any consistent fill.

**Verdict: the puzzle is not uniquely solvable as printed.** The intended reading
(1D=SAFAR at cell 1) supports a 12-clue fill — 10 of the 12 printed clues plus the two
scene-clued entries — leaving 4D and 6A empty and "prices & wrists" and "Nap"
unplaced. That is structural (too few compatible slots), not a transcription defect,
and it is further evidence for the r/themoneyhunt community's objection that the book
embeds incomplete information.

*Curiosity:* an alternative fill also places 12 clues — 2A=SCRAP, 3A=AMORT, 5A=DEAR,
6A=MARKS, 6D=MEET, 7D=**RAJAB**, 8A=NEAT, 9D=EEK, 10A=**BAR**, 11D=RIVERHORSE,
12A=DOCK, 13A=ICING — but it leaves cell **1 empty** (the month sits at 7D) and strands
"Blackjack term" and "Nap". Recorded for completeness; the SAFAR reading above is the
intended one.
