## Corrections this transcription makes to earlier docs

1. **`docs/money-hunt-status-2026-10-30.md` open thread #2 and
   `recommended_next_steps.md` §C are wrong about structure.** They describe
   p11/p22/p23 as "segments of the big p25 top-hat crossword's clue set". They are
   not — the same numbers carry *different* clue text on p25 (e.g. 42 ACROSS "Most
   undesirable part" vs 42 DOWN "Cleans"). p11/22/23 are the DOWN list; p25 is
   ACROSS.

   list skips 95–97 and `money-hunt-status-2026-10-30.md` calls it "a page defect".
   They are DOWN-only numbers and are printed here on page 22:
   95 Subarctic Russian forest (TAIGA) · 96 Hayworth and Rio (RITAS) · 97 Angry.

   clue lists are transcribed" was false.** The lists had never been transcribed;
   pages 10/11/22/23 were withdrawn stubs. They are transcribed now (grades above).
