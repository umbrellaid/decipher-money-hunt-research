# Repeated-motif survey of the 24 Money Hunt path pages (2026-10-10)

**Purpose.** Test the wishkohaku "deduction = subtraction" mechanism
([mapping record](../data/money_hunt_wishkohaku_mapping.md)): every path page
(5–28) should contain an illustration photocopied from another page with exactly
one element removed/changed; the page's letter is the initial of the removed
element. Method: view all 24 pages at survey resolution, inventory every
element that appears on ≥ 2 pages, then diff the strongest candidate pairs at
native 300-dpi resolution.

**Working notes** (private workspace, not published): full per-page inventory
in `survey_notes.md` under the money-hunt puzzle folder.

## Verified (carried over from the mapping doc)

- **p13 → L**: plain parking-sign P (p13) vs P with diagonal line (p20, two
  copies). Missing element "line" → L. Confirmed at 300 dpi.
- **p28 → I**: bookshelf spine reads "VA DERS" = INVADERS missing "IN" → I.
  Survived 4× re-reading; the page's other misspellings are substitutions,
  this is the only clean subtraction.

## Claimed pairs tested and failed

- **"Milk box on 7 and 18" (YouTube comment motif):** p7 has the words
  "Whole Milk" floating in a speech circle with **no carton drawn at all**
  (native-res crop, p7). On p18 (general-store page) **no milk box or milk
  reference was found** in left/right/top native crops — jars read Candy,
  Cookies, Jelly Beans; bag reads "staying". The pair as described does not
  exist. (If the mechanism applies to p7/p18, the duplicated element is
  something else, or the comment misremembered the page.)
- **"Same armchair on 17 and 19":** both pages show a striped easy chair, but
  native crops show they are **different illustrations** — p17 is a wing-back
  chair + ottoman in a matching striped suite with a garment draped over it;
  p19 is a single worn tub chair with narrow stripes and patched arms. Not a
  photocopy pair.

## What this means for the mapping

- After surveying all 24 pages, **only the 2 previously verified letters hold**.
- Two motif claims circulating in the YouTube comments (milk box 7/18, armchair
  17/19) **do not survive native-res checking** — a caution against trusting
  comment-sourced motif lists without crops.
- The mechanism is not *disproven* — survey resolution can miss small repeated
  clip-art, and ~6 candidate pairs remain untested — but the hit rate so far
  (2/24 verified, 2 tested claims false) means the "US NY THE EMPIRE STATE
  BUILDING" reading stays **unproven**, and the convenient "US NY" split
  (letting pages 9 and 15 each supply two letters) remains its weakest point.
- Cheapest next tests: native-res bird diff (p12/p14), horse diff (p22/p23),
  and a careful p25 scan for the top hat.
