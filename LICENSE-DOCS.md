## What this repository deliberately does NOT contain

- No scans or photographs of the physical puzzles, rulebooks, newspapers or
  the Money Hunt clue book. Those images belong to Karen Kavett (and, for the
  clue book, to the original publisher); link to her shared folders instead
  (see `docs/sources.md`).
- No full text of any in-copyright book. The solver only ever reads texts you
  supply yourself from your own lawful copies.
- No private correspondence, personal prompts, or anyone's contact details
  beyond what was published in 1980s–90s print and is quoted here as history.
